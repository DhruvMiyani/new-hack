# Referral eFax workstation

The interaction reference is the [eClinicalWorks fax inbox training guide](https://irp.cdn-website.com/b4ec96c9/files/uploaded/Retrieving-Faxes-Fax-Plug-In-Mode-%282%29.pdf).
Pages 1–2 show a Documents navigation band, facility selector, incoming rows,
a preview of the selected fax, and patient assignment. The
[official eClinicalWorks EHR page](https://www.eclinicalworks.com/products-services/ehr/)
describes inbound fax review, and the official site capture showed dark navy
navigation, blue action accents, and light blue surfaces. The scanned training
guide showed thin gray row dividers and a blue active selection.

Use those patterns as a visual reference for this original demo. The exact
palette, spacing and split panes are implementation choices. Use a navy app
bar, light blue active row, cool gray document surround, compact system type,
and a white paper preview. Put the facility and fax metadata above the
worklist. Keep county acceptance and clinical review separate in the decision
pane. The preview must correspond to the highlighted fax. Make source links
and keyboard controls available. Preserve the scan, extracted text, and MRI
as separate views. At narrow widths stack the worklist above the preview.

The real [UCSF Neurology referral workflow](https://www.ucsfhealth.org/locations/neurology-complex-diagnosis-mission-bay/refer)
and [VA Form 10-10172](https://www.va.gov/forms/10-10172/) informed the fax
packet. Do not include product branding or patient data from reference
screenshots. The app and office are fictional, and the VA-derived first
page carries a training watermark.
